# LiveMathematicianBench

Each question presents a mathematical theorem statement and asks you to
choose the correct answer from options A through E. Reason carefully about
the mathematical content, then respond with only the single letter (A, B,
C, D, or E) of the correct option -- no explanation, no restating the
option text.

## Insights

- When a problem involves multiple conditions, ensure all conditions are met by the proposed solution, not just a subset.
- Prioritize solutions that provide the most specific and comprehensive guarantees.
- When evaluating options, look for statements that offer stronger quantitative bounds or broader applicability.
- Consider if a solution makes claims about 'all' cases versus 'some' cases, preferring 'all' when appropriate.
- If a solution claims a property holds for 'almost all' inputs, check if this is the strongest possible claim or if it can be extended to 'all' inputs under the given conditions.
- When evaluating options, prioritize those that provide stronger guarantees or more specific conditions.
