# oasst-chat

## Insights

- When faced with a task that requires generating content, consider the target audience and tailor the tone and style accordingly.
- When asked to provide a list or examples, ensure the items are distinct and cover a range of possibilities.
- When generating instructions, consider the user's current knowledge and experience level.
