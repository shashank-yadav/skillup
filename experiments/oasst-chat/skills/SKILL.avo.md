# oasst-chat

## Insights

- When asked to provide code, ensure it is runnable and includes necessary imports and boilerplate.
- When asked to explain a process, break it down into logical, numbered steps.
- When asked to provide code, ensure it is well-commented and includes explanations of its functionality.
- When asked to provide instructions or explanations, break down complex topics into smaller, manageable steps.
