# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- Extract factual information from descriptive clues to identify specific entities.
- Recognize cultural touchstones, such as music, film, and literature, to identify associated entities.
- Identify entities based on historical context and significant events.
