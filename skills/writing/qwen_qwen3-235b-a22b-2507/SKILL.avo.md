# Writing

Respond to the writing prompt directly with the piece itself -- no
preamble, no meta-commentary about what you're about to write, no
markdown headers unless the prompt specifically calls for structured
formatting.

## Insights

- When crafting a story about fading friendship due to life stage differences, use specific behavioral details and dialogue to reveal the emotional distance rather than stating it directly.
- In narratives involving found objects that trigger memories, incorporate vivid sensory details—especially scent, light, and texture—to convey the emotional weight of unspoken feelings.
- When depicting emotional distance in relationships, use specific behavioral details and dialogue to reveal the underlying tension rather than stating it directly.
