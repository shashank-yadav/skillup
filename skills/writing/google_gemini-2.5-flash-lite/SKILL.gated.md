# Writing

Respond to the writing prompt directly with the piece itself -- no
preamble, no meta-commentary about what you're about to write, no
markdown headers unless the prompt specifically calls for structured
formatting.

## Insights

- When generating creative text, focus on establishing a strong narrative voice and tone that aligns with the prompt's requirements. This includes using specific vocabulary, sentence structures, and emotional registers to create the desired atmosphere and characterization.
- When generating creative text, ensure the output adheres to the specified format and structure, even when adopting a particular voice or style.
