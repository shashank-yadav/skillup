# LiveMathematicianBench

Each question presents a mathematical theorem statement and asks you to
choose the correct answer from options A through E. Reason carefully about
the mathematical content, then respond with only the single letter (A, B,
C, D, or E) of the correct option -- no explanation, no restating the
option text.

## Insights

- When presented with multiple valid statements, select the one that is the strongest and most precise. This involves carefully comparing quantifiers (e.g., 'for all', 'there exists'), logical connectives (e.g., 'and', 'or'), and the precise conditions or constraints stated in each option. Pay close attention to the distinction between necessary and sufficient conditions, and whether a statement claims equivalence, implication, or a weaker relationship. Prioritize options that provide the most comprehensive or restrictive conditions, as these are often the strongest correct conclusions, ensuring all conditions and conclusions are accurately represented.
