# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- Answer with the most specific and concise term that directly matches the clue
- Extract the specific entity being asked for by focusing on the unique descriptors in the clue
- When a clue contains a quote or phrase in quotes, consider it a direct reference to a title, name, or well-known expression
