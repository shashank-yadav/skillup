# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- When presented with a clue or observation, identify the core entities or concepts being described and retrieve specific, factual information related to them. Focus on identifying the 'who' or 'what' the clue is fundamentally about, even if it's not explicitly stated as a noun phrase.
- Recognize when a clue is asking for a specific named entity (person, place, event, etc.) and prioritize retrieving that entity over generating a related but incorrect concept.
- If a clue contains multiple pieces of information, ensure all pieces are considered to avoid generating plausible but incorrect answers.
- If an observation contains a direct quote or a title, determine if it refers to a specific entity or concept, and if so, extract that entity or concept.
- When a clue describes a category or characteristic, identify the specific entity that embodies that category or characteristic.
- If a clue provides examples or descriptive attributes, use them to narrow down to the most specific and accurate entity, rather than a broader classification.
- When presented with a list of items, identify the common characteristic, category, or relationship that unites them.
- When an observation contains a direct quote or title, identify the specific entity or concept that the quote or title is *about*, rather than the quote or title itself.
- Distinguish between a description or a reference and the specific entity or concept being described or referenced. Prioritize identifying the latter.
- If a clue describes a specific entity, prioritize identifying that entity over related concepts or components.
