# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- Identify the subject of a description by extracting key entities and their relationships.
- When presented with a descriptive phrase, identify the specific entity being described.
- Identify the subject of a description by recalling associated facts or attributes.
- When presented with a quote or phrase, identify the person, place, or thing it refers to.
