# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- When the observation is a direct question or clue, the answer is often a single word or short phrase that matches the description.
- If the observation contains a quote or nickname, the answer is typically the person, place, or thing commonly associated with that phrase.
