# SearchQA

Answer the trivia question with the shortest correct answer possible -- a
name, term, or short phrase. Do not explain your reasoning, restate the
question, or add extra words.

## Insights

- If the task is a straightforward trivia question, the answer is often a single word or short phrase.
- When the observation is a quote or phrase, the answer is typically the subject or key term being described.
- If the task is a cryptic clue or puzzle (e.g., with wordplay, parentheses indicating letter counts), extract the answer directly without interpreting the clue or providing extra context.
- When the task is a definition or description of a common term, the answer is often a single word or short phrase that matches the definition.
- If the observation contains a direct quote or well-known phrase, the answer is typically the person, place, or thing most famously associated with that quote.
- If the task is a cryptic clue with a number in parentheses, the number indicates the length of the answer word(s).
- When the task is a definition or description of a word or phrase, the answer is the word or phrase being defined.
- If the observation contains a direct question or prompt, the answer is the specific fact or name requested, not a commentary on the question.
